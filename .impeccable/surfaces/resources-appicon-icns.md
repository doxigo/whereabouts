# Whereabouts mark

Scope: the brand mark across its three carriers: macOS app icon (`Resources/AppIcon.icns`), the menu-bar status glyph (`StampGlyph.swift`), and the wordmark lockup for the README and site (`Branding/`). Mode: Persuade (first contact is a GitHub README; the icon also fronts every alert notification).

Audience and job: Mac users on a VPN or proxy who need to know where the internet thinks they are. The mark must say "place, verified" at a glance and stay legible from 16px to 1024px.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every check is an entry stamp: the internet admits you somewhere and Whereabouts inks where. Refuses the category default (shield, padlock, globe with a pin on a blue gradient) and the old metal compass.

OWN-WORLD: Passport visa page: pale mint security tint with fine guilloché, violet rubber-stamp ink with uneven pressure and speckle, a tilted rounded-rect entry stamp with a double border. Lettering is grid-built condensed monoline caps like customs type. Inks carry meaning: violet = brand at rest, green admitted, red refused, orange leak.

STORY: A visitor sees a fresh entry stamp, an arrow arriving at a point, and reads "this tells me where I've landed". In the menu bar the same stamp frame holds the verdict: the arrow into a point when admitted, a knocked-out !, drop or × when wrong.

FIRST VIEWPORT: App icon at 1024: squircle page, stamp centred and tilted -7°, about 80% of the squircle width (85% tilted bounds; adapted from 70% so the lettering stays legible at the 128px slot), WHEREABOUTS across its top, arrow-into-point at its centre, ADMITTED beneath; two faint older green stamps carrying place codes (PT · LIS, NL · AMS) bleed off the edges. 16–64px drop lettering, guilloché and ghosts, thicken the frame.

FORM: Port of Entry (border-control entry stamps), position 4 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key 4bd8d7f1. Raises: half-inked stamp for checking (algorave); one measured grid for icon, glyph and wordmark (oscilloscope); unknown/offline keep an empty frame (toy shelf); one ink, one law (gravity rain); faint earlier impressions as history (coiled tower).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
